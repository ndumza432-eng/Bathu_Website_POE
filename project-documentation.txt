BATHU POE DOCUMENTATION
Project goal: Create a functional and visually appealing website for Bathu.
Target audience: Young adults, students, young professionals, sneaker enthusiasts and South African online shoppers.
Pages: Home, About Us, Products, Enquiry and Contact.
Technologies: HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
Requirements: responsive design, clear navigation, product presentation, product search/filtering, enquiry validation and multiple store locations.
