ABOUT BATHU

Our Story

Learn about the organisation behind the brand.

History

Bathu was founded in 2015 by Theo Baloyi in Alexandra Township, South Africa. The business developed from a local entrepreneurial idea into a recognised South African footwear and lifestyle brand.

The brand is associated with African identity, entrepreneurship, innovation and the idea that everyone has their own journey.

Mission

To provide stylish, quality footwear while celebrating African identity and creating meaningful opportunities.

Vision

To grow as a leading African footwear and lifestyle brand with a strong local identity.

Values

Innovation, quality, pride, entrepreneurship, community and perseverance.

Founder

Theo Baloyi is the founder of Bathu. His entrepreneurial journey forms an important part of the organisation's story.
